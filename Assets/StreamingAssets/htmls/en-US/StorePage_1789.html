The Last Hero: 1789

You are right — The Last Hero: 1789 is an original side\-scrolling pixel game developed by Raji Net. The game unfolds in a whimsical place called Toilet Village, where the chosen are granted a Plunger Rod to channel the power of the Red Stone. You play as a mysterious figure known as the Last Hero, meeting companions with distinct personalities and unique abilities during a free\-ranging journey, fighting formidable foes together and avenging the Demon King — while gradually uncovering the truth behind the Red Stone.
